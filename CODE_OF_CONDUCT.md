# Code of conduct

## Our community

Jano welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Treat
people with respect regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity,
nationality, appearance, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, give constructive feedback, accept corrections, and focus on
ideas and code. Help newcomers and respect other people's time and boundaries.
Disagreement is welcome when expressed respectfully.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention or content, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without
permission are unacceptable.

## Scope and responsibility

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, reviews and other community
spaces, including public interactions when representing the project.
Maintainers must apply these rules fairly and protect reporters' privacy.

## Reporting

The maintainer is [Rodrigo Rangel (@Hangell)](https://github.com/Hangell).
A dedicated private project contact has not yet been designated. For abuse on
GitHub, use its built-in reporting and blocking tools. If a private contact is
listed on the maintainer's profile, it may also be used for project reports.
Do not post sensitive incidents or personal information in public issues.

Include links to the interaction, what happened, and any relevant context when
reporting through an available private channel. Maintainers should share report
information only as needed to investigate and respond.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and recurrence, maintainers may request a correction,
issue a warning, remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or impose
a permanent ban. Threats or serious harassment may warrant an immediate ban.
Affected participants may request reconsideration through the same available
private channel, providing new context. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
